## Files and lines

1. **New `app/src/main/res/drawable/bg_top_scrim.xml`:**

   ```xml
   <shape xmlns:android="http://schemas.android.com/apk/res/android" android:shape="rectangle">
       <gradient
           android:angle="270"
           android:startColor="#99000000"
           android:endColor="#00000000"
           android:type="linear" />
   </shape>
   ```

   `angle="270"` runs top → bottom: 60 % black at the top edge, fully transparent at the bottom edge. No
   `centerColor`, so the alpha falls linearly and the numbers below hold.

2. **`app/src/main/res/layout/activity_main.xml`:** insert directly **after** `previewView` (`:9-14`) and
   **before** `safeZone` (`:16-19`):

   ```xml
   <View
       android:id="@+id/topScrim"
       android:layout_width="0dp"
       android:layout_height="120dp"
       android:background="@drawable/bg_top_scrim"
       android:importantForAccessibility="no"
       app:layout_constraintEnd_toEndOf="parent"
       app:layout_constraintStart_toStartOf="parent"
       app:layout_constraintTop_toTopOf="parent" />
   ```

3. **`app/src/main/res/layout/view_review.xml`:** the same view with `android:id="@+id/reviewTopScrim"`, inserted
   directly **after** `playerView` (`:9-15`) and **before** `reviewSafeZone` (`:17-20`).

No change to `MainActivity.kt`, `themes.xml`, strings or any other drawable.

### Z-order (why directly above the video)

ConstraintLayout draws children in XML order. Placing the scrim right after the `PreviewView` / `PlayerView`
darkens only the camera or video image:

- `safeZone` / `reviewSafeZone` (guide lines), `gestureView` (`OverlayGestureView` draws the user's image/text
  overlays and their handles, `overlay/OverlayGestureView.kt:338`) and `captionView` (burned-in captions) stay
  **above** the scrim. They show what will be in the exported video; darkening them would misrepresent the result.
- All controls come later in the XML and stay above the scrim.
- The scrim is a plain `View` without click listener (`clickable=false`), so touches pass through to
  `gestureView` and the controls as today.
- The scrim is UI only; recording (CameraX) and export (`Exporter.kt`) never see it.

### Height

120 dp, as in the concept. It covers the top control band on both screens (measured from the layout on
`0c01699`, below the PR-1 inset): record – `segmentBar` 12–18 dp, `statusText` from 32 dp to about 64 dp at font
1.0, `settingsButton` (44 dp, 12 dp below `statusText`) about 76–120 dp; review – `reviewBar` 12–18 dp, `backButton`/`saveDraftButton`/`reviewStatus`
32–84 dp. At font 200 % the status pills grow below 84 dp (PR-5 allows two lines); they keep their own `bg_pill`,
so readability does not depend on the scrim there. The right rail (`rightBar`, tile buttons up to 294 dp) and the
captions column extend below the scrim; they have their own round backgrounds (`#66000000`) and are not part of
A20.

## Gradient approach and contrast (worst case: pure white footage)

Composite of black layers over white (`#FFFFFF`), WCAG contrast of white content against it:

| Position from scrim top | Scrim alpha | White on scrim only | White text on `bg_pill` (`#66000000`) + scrim |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 dp | 0.60 | 5.74 : 1 | 10.8 : 1 |
| 12–18 dp (segment bars) | 0.54–0.51 | 4.6–4.1 : 1 | – |
| 48 dp (status pills, buttons centre) | 0.36 | 2.5 : 1 | 6.1 : 1 |
| 84 dp (bottom of back/save buttons) | 0.18 | 1.5 : 1 | 4.1 : 1 |
| without scrim (today) | 0 | 1.0 : 1 | **2.85 : 1** |

- The white segments of `segmentBar`/`reviewBar` reach ≥ 4.1 : 1 (WCAG non-text needs 3 : 1); with the PR-3
  outline they are visible even without the scrim, so the two measures add up and do not conflict.
- White text in the grey pills goes from 2.85 : 1 (fails AA) to ≥ 4.1 : 1 across the button band and ≥ 6 : 1 at
  the pill centre (passes AA 4.5 : 1 for the 14 sp status text).
- On dark footage the scrim changes almost nothing visible (black on black).
- Rejected alternative: a solid band (`#66000000`, 120 dp). It gives a hard edge across the video and hides more of
  the picture; the concept asks for a "leichter dunkler Verlauf".
- Rejected alternative: `scrimColor`/`MaterialToolbar` or `WindowInsetsController` tricks – there is no toolbar,
  and the status bar is handled by PR-1.

## Interaction with A1 (PR-1 inset padding)

PR-1 pads `binding.root` with `systemBars() or displayCutout()` and does **not** call `enableEdgeToEdge()`. Effect
for PR-6:

- **API 35/36:** the root content starts below the status bar (black strip from `windowBackground`, light icons).
  `topScrim` is constrained to the parent top, so it starts **at the bottom of that strip**, exactly where
  `segmentBar`/`reviewBar` start. The status-bar icons stay on black; `isAppearanceLightStatusBars` needs no change.
- **API 26–34:** the root padding is 0; the scrim starts at the top of the content area below the opaque system
  bar. Same look on every API level – no per-API code.
- **Why no `MainActivity.kt` change:** the brief expected the insets area of `MainActivity.kt`. Because the scrim is
  a child of the padded root, the PR-1 listener positions it for free; adding the top inset to its height in code
  would only be needed for a full-bleed layout.
- **Rejected alternative: full-bleed video with the scrim under the status bar** (the concept text "Das Video bleibt
  Vollbild"). In code it is small at the top: only `segmentBar` (`activity_main.xml:34`) and `reviewBar`
  (`view_review.xml:47`) are anchored to the parent top, every other top control chains from them
  (`statusText` → `segmentBar`, `settingsButton`/rail/tile buttons → `statusText`, review buttons → `reviewBar`).
  The PR-1 listener would apply left/right/bottom to the root and the top inset as extra `topMargin` on these two
  bars plus extra height on the scrim. It is still rejected for 1.1.6 because:
  - the `PreviewView`/`PlayerView` would grow by the status-bar height **on API 35/36 only**. `gestureView` and
    `safeZone` map overlays and guides through `frameAspect` relative to their own size
    (`MainActivity.kt:292-295`), so overlay placement and the `fillCenter` crop would differ between Android 14 and
    15/16 – a WYSIWYG risk on the camera screen that needs its own test round;
  - it reopens PR-1's verified inset approach after merge, and the status-bar icons would then depend on the scrim
    over white footage (5.7 : 1 at alpha 0.6, enough, but one more thing to verify per API level).
  **Consequence for the owner:** on Android 15/16 the video in 1.1.6 ends below a black status-bar strip (as on
  Android 14 today) instead of running under the clock. If the owner wants the full-bleed look, it becomes its own
  small plan for 1.1.7 or later (the two-bar `topMargin` change above plus an overlay-mapping check).

## Interaction with A22 (PR-3 outline)

- PR-3 draws a 1 dp 55 % black outline around the track and finished segments (`SegmentProgressView.kt`). Under
  the scrim the outline is darker than its background only by a little (bar zone alpha ≈ 0.5); it stays harmless,
  as the PR-3 plan says ("the A22 outline stays harmless under a scrim and does not need to be removed").
- **Keep the outline.** The scrim fades with height and depends on the footage; the outline makes the bar visible
  on any background, also where the scrim is weak (wide cutouts, future layout moves). Removing it would be a
  separate design decision with a new screenshot round.
- `SegmentProgressView.kt` is not touched by PR-6.

## JVM tests

- **No new JVM test.** PR-6 is two layout views and one drawable; there is no logic and no Robolectric in the
  project. A layout-parsing test (as `TouchTargetLayoutTest` in PR-4) would only restate the XML.
- Existing tests stay green: `StringsConsistencyTest` (no new strings), `ReviewTimeTest` (PR-3),
  `TouchTargetLayoutTest` (PR-4; the scrim is not clickable and has no 48 dp rule), `StatusTextTest` (PR-5).
- `./gradlew assembleDebug testDebugUnitTest lintDebug` green; lint must not report `Overdraw` as an error (the
  scrim is one extra translucent layer on a 120 dp band; acceptable, a warning at most).

## Acceptance criteria

1. On API 35 and 36, in en, fa and ar, on the bright scene: the segment/review bar, the status pills and the
   back, save-draft and settings icons are readable; the pill background measures darker than today.
2. The gradient starts at the bottom of the status-bar strip (API 35/36) or the top of the content (API 30), has no
   visible hard edge, and ends at 120 dp.
3. User overlays, safe-zone guides and captions are not darkened; recordings and exports are unchanged (compare
   one exported frame with a 1.1.5 export of the same scene, or state that export was not touched by code review).
4. Touches in the top 120 dp reach `gestureView` and the controls as before (move a text overlay that sits in the
   top band).
5. No change outside `activity_main.xml`, `view_review.xml` and the new `bg_top_scrim.xml`; no new strings; no
   `app/build.gradle.kts` change.
6. `build-test-lint` and `pages-check` green.

## versionCode rule for 1.1.6 / 1.1.7 (planner resolution, 2026-11-14)

This closes the open point in `docs/DECISIONS.md` (2026-11-13, "one versionCode 85 for the whole cycle vs. every
build +1"):

- **85 = the first 1.1.6 store build.** PR-1 sets `versionCode = 85` and `versionName = "1.1.6"`.
- **Every further build that goes to testers is +1** (86, 87, …). This is the existing rule in
  `docs/KNOWLEDGE.md` ("Bump `versionCode` (+1) for every build that goes to testers").
- **PR-2 to PR-6 do not bump** unless a tester build is cut from them; debug builds from `build.yml` that do not go
  to testers keep 85. A PR that is the source of a new tester build bumps by one in that PR.
- **1.1.7 takes the next free code after the last 1.1.6 tester build**, not a fixed 86. This supersedes "1.1.7 /
  versionCode 86" in hub knowledge #2753 and in
  [`2026-11-14-1-1-6-design-polish.md`](2026-11-14-1-1-6-design-polish.md) ("Out of scope"); 86 is right only if
  no second 1.1.6 tester build is needed.

PR-6 itself does not touch `app/build.gradle.kts`.

## Design rules applied

- **Concept item 1** (owner-approved): "leichter dunkler Verlauf", 120 dp, behind the top controls.
- **ui-cheatsheet "text on images → scrim/gradient"** and **apple-design legibility/contrast** (cited by the
  audit for A20): gradient instead of a solid band, measured contrast instead of "looks fine".
- **Restraint:** no new colour, no new control, no motion; the scrim is static and does not animate with
  recording state.
